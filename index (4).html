Credibility is about incentives.

A threat or promise is credible only if following through is actually Player 2’s best response when the moment comes—or if the alternative has been removed.

Statements are not strategic moves by themselves.

Saying “I will follow through” changes nothing unless the statement alters payoffs, removes an option, or otherwise changes the game.

Credible ≠ necessarily effective.

A credible move matters only if it changes Player 1’s incentives enough to alter the choice they make at the start.
